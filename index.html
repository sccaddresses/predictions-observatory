The operating system

Data → observation → model → market → decision.

The architecture refuses to blur these stages. That separation is how we can distinguish independent sporting signal from market imitation and post-event storytelling.

Research architecture

Built to become more intelligent, not merely more complicated.

The roadmap prioritises richer evidence before model glamour: course geometry, pace, human connections, weather, longitudinal result learning and calibrated market comparison.

⌁

Course DNA

Direction, turns, straight length, undulation, gradients, draw interaction and course similarity as a reusable geometry layer.

↝

Pace & race shape

Front-runners, prominent racers, midfield and held-up profiles become expected pressure — not a paragraph added after the finish.

◎

Human engine

Trainer × course, jockey × course, horse × jockey and stable context can be measured with proper shrinkage instead of anecdote.

☂

Weather & going

Time-stamped rainfall, wind, temperature and going evolution can be connected to course geometry and runner evidence.

∿

Probability quality

Calibration curves, Brier score and log loss matter more than simply counting winners once models publish full probability fields.

↔

Market discrepancy

Market prices remain separate evidence so disagreement can be studied rather than quietly baked into a supposedly independent model.
