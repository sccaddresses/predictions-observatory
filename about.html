Project vision

A prediction lab, not a crystal ball.

SCC PREDICTIONS is being built as reusable forecasting infrastructure: independent models, point-in-time data, uncertainty, provenance, market comparison and honest forward evaluation — first in horse racing, then in other domains where the same discipline can be tested.

Why it exists

Prediction is easy to claim after the event.

The project is designed around a simple defence against hindsight: preserve what was known, preserve what was predicted, then let the result score it. That makes misses as valuable to the research record as winners.

SCC Nexus discipline

Search. Corroborate. Communicate.

PREDICTIONS applies the wider SCC Nexus approach to forecasting: acquire evidence, corroborate signals and communicate uncertainty without converting data coverage into certainty.

What makes it different

The interesting bit is not the pick. It is the experiment.

A conventional prediction page optimises for confidence. This project optimises for traceability: what input existed, which model used it, what probability or evidence share was produced, when it was frozen and how it performed later.

Prediction artefacts stay immutable.
Market prices remain a comparator, not an invisible crutch.
Probability quality is scored where a full field distribution was frozen prospectively.
Missing and failed outcomes remain visible.
Learning creates a new version, never a rewritten past.
Current research status

Forward validation, not production certainty.

Current race percentages are experimental or provisional. Historical walk-forward training, richer Course DNA, pace, weather and connection features remain development priorities. The website is deliberately explicit about that boundary because credibility is more valuable than pretending an early model is finished.

For reviewers

Challenge the method, not just the outcome.

The most useful external review focuses on leakage, feature validity, sample size, model calibration, benchmark choice, runner matching and whether the public claims remain inside the evidence actually collected.

Public artefacts

Evidence designed to be inspectable.

Race reports, post-race evaluations and the public dashboard are generated from repository artefacts rather than hand-edited outcome stories.
